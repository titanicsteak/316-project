CSC316 · Data centres and the race for land and power

AI is starving for power.
These are the places feeding it.

Every chatbot reply runs in a building somewhere, on a piece of land, drawing from a local grid. Those buildings are going up right now, and where they go is being decided in county meetings most people never hear about.

The pipeline is 235,357 megawatts of planned capacity against 16,364 megawatts operating today. That is not a forecast. Those are sites with names, owners and coordinates, already filed.

Growth and scale come apart. Maryland, Oklahoma and Idaho are adding computing-infrastructure businesses faster than anywhere else, but the megawatts are going to Texas and Virginia, which were already the biggest. Georgia is the only state near the top of both lists.

Somebody will own all of this. Somebody already does.
